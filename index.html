02 — EVERYDAY

Accounting for people who don’t do accounting.

Not everyone on your team speaks debits and credits. The Everyday tab lets anyone record what happened in plain language — Ambit writes the correct double-entry behind the scenes and routes it to your accountant to approve.

  • “I paid for something”, “I made a sale”, “I paid in advance”
  • Even prepaids and accruals are booked in the right month
  • Nothing posts until an accountant reviews and approves it

03 — CASH CONVERSION CYCLE

See the cash tied up in your business.

Ambit computes your cash conversion cycle straight from the ledger — how long cash is locked in inventory and receivables, less what you owe suppliers. Watch it move as you collect faster, hold less stock or renegotiate terms.

  • DIO + DSO − DPO, recalculated on every posting
  • Pairs with cash runway, burn and a 13-week forecast
  • The working-capital number investors ask for, always ready
